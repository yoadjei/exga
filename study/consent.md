## What is this study about?

When researchers combine the results of many studies, they copy numbers such as sample sizes
out of each paper by hand. This takes time and errors happen. exga is a tool that reads the
papers with a language model, proposes the numbers, and marks which ones it can vouch for at
a stated error level. This study asks how long the work takes and how accurate it is with and
without exga, and what people think of using it.

## What will I do?

The session takes about 60 minutes. After a short demonstration you will do two tasks: find
a few values in three research papers by hand, and find the same kind of values in three
other papers with exga. Each task takes at most 25 minutes. Then you answer a short
questionnaire about using the tool. The researcher records how long each task takes.

Nothing is audio or video recorded, and your screen is not recorded. You are not tested: the
study looks at the tool, not at you.

## Do I have to take part?

No. Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without giving a reason. You can also
ask for your data to be deleted until ____, after which the results are combined and
individual data can no longer be removed. Deciding not to take part, or to stop, has no
effect on your studies, marks or work.

## Are there risks or benefits?

The tasks are ordinary computer work; there are no known risks beyond that. There is no
direct benefit to you, but the results will help show whether tools like this make data
extraction faster or more reliable.

## What happens to my data?

- You are known in the data only by an id such as P03. Your name is only on this consent
  form, which is stored separately from the data.
- The data are your task times, the values you found, and your questionnaire answers. They
  are kept on the researcher's password-protected computer and are not shared.
- Results are reported for the group (for example average times) in the researcher's thesis
  and any related publication. No one can be identified from them.
- If you agree below, short anonymous quotes from your written answers may be used.
- The data are kept until ____ and then deleted.
